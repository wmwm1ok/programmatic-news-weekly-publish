一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • CTV 已从增量频道升级为程序化广告的核心增长与能力建设战场——平台同时争夺优质库存、自助工具和创意优化。例如 Magnite 与 Teads 的 CTV 收入高增，Teads 接入 TiVo Ads，Walmart 收购 Vibe.co。
  • 跨媒体测量正向“质量验证+受众触达+效果评估”一体化收敛——广告主不再接受割裂指标。例如 Nielsen 收购 DoubleVerify，LiveRamp 将 Meta 纳入 Cross-Media Intelligence，Amazon Ads 加入 Screenforce。
  • 零售媒体正从站内广告位扩展为全域商业媒体网络，但增长质量分化加剧。例如 Academy Sports 上线 RMN，Walmart 收购 Vibe.co 并推出 Brand Lift，Criteo 扩大 Loblaw 合作却受客户缩量拖累。
  • AI 正从投放优化模型下沉为广告交易的操作与治理层——行业开始关注代理权限、持续学习和自动化执行。例如 PubMatic 发布 AgenticOS 治理架构，Moloco 解析 CARA，Vibe 推出 CTV 投放 CLI。
  • 去 Cookie 路线正从浏览器统一替代方案转向认证流量与平台内受众补强。例如 Google 终止 Privacy Sandbox，ATS 已有大量出版商接入，Eyeota 与 Zeotap 为 Amazon DSP 补充受众方案。
  • CTV has moved from an incremental channel to a core growth and capability battleground for programmatic advertising, with platforms competing on premium supply, self-serve tools, and creative optimization. For example, Magnite and Teads reported strong CTV growth, Teads integrated TiVo Ads, and Walmart acquired Vibe.co.
  • Cross-media measurement is converging toward an integrated stack of quality verification, audience reach, and outcome evaluation, as advertisers push back against fragmented metrics. For example, Nielsen is acquiring DoubleVerify, LiveRamp added Meta to Cross-Media Intelligence, and Amazon Ads joined Screenforce measurement.
  • Retail media is expanding from onsite ad placements into full commerce media networks, while growth quality is becoming more uneven. For example, Academy Sports launched an RMN, Walmart acquired Vibe.co and added Brand Lift capabilities, while Criteo expanded with Loblaw but disclosed pressure from client reductions.
  • AI is moving beyond bidding optimization into the operating and governance layer of ad transactions, with more focus on agent permissions, continuous learning, and automated execution. For example, PubMatic launched AgenticOS governance, Moloco detailed CARA, and Vibe introduced a CLI for CTV campaign management.
  • Cookieless strategy is shifting from a browser-level universal replacement toward authenticated traffic and platform-level audience enrichment. For example, Google ended Privacy Sandbox, ATS has gained broad publisher adoption, and Eyeota and Zeotap added audience solutions to Amazon DSP.

Google 终止 Privacy Sandbox 计划 Programmatic of Tomorrow or a DSP Utopia: Privacy Sandbox Explained

Google 叫停 Privacy Sandbox，这项旨在替代第三方 Cookie、内置于 Chrome 的隐私广告技术路线正式退场，意味着浏览器端去 Cookie 方案再次生变。

TL;DR:The Privacy Sandbox was Google's six-year project to replace third-party cookies with privacy-preserving ad technologies built into the Google Chrome browser. Google officially discontinued the initiative in October 2025 after retiring most of its core APIs, including Topics, Protected Audience, and Attribution Reporting, citing low adoption and continued regulatory pressure. Third-party cookies will remain in Chrome indefinitely, but privacy compliance obligations under GDPR and CCPA remain unchanged. The industry is shifting toward first-party data strategies and contextual advertising as the practical path forward. For DSPs, the programmatic buying mechanics that existed before the Privacy Sandbox will continue to operate, but advertisers and publishers need to invest in their own data infrastructure rather than waiting for a platform-level solution that no longer exists."We've made the decision to maintain our current approach to offering users third-party cookie choice in Chrome."That was Google's statement on April 22, 2025. Six months later, on October 17, 2025, Google retired most of the Privacy Sandbox APIs it had spent six years building.The initiative launched in 2019 with a clear goal: replace cross-site tracking with privacy-preserving alternatives that keptad techand digital advertising functional. It ended with a quiet blog post, a long retirement list of APIs, and an industry largely back where it started.According to aMarch 2025 Deloitte report, only15%of global marketers felt fully prepared for a cookieless environment, despite years of warnings. The Privacy Sandbox was supposed to solve that. It did not.

Authenticated Traffic Solution (ATS) –a privacy-focused, content-driven user authentication solution, that brands and publishers can employ to collect first-party user data. Some 215 publishers and 25 SSPs worldwide have adopted ATS.RampID (former IdentityLink )– a determinative, people-based user ID graph.LiveRamp positions itself as the largest deterministic identity graph on the market with PII accumulated from 245 million Americans .The main principles of Ramp IDIn 2020, LiveRamp partnered with The Trade Desk to join its universal identity development efforts.This partnership enabled buyers to bid on The Trade Desk with Ramp ID and maintain measurement across multiple environments – desktop, mobile, and connected TV. Essentially, brands can maintain effective attribution, measurement, and ROI without any third-party cookies or device IDs. Publishers, in turn, can also maintain high CPMs and demonstrate better ROAs while ensuring high consumer privacy.Main benefits of user ID graphs Access to compliant, first-party data based user profiles for advanced targeting and programmatic ad campaignsHigh scalability potential with multi-device and multichannel reachMultiple types of user ID solutions available on the marketPrecise, cross-environment measurement and attributionCapable of real-time personalization and dynamic, context-sensitive creativeMajor drawbacks of user ID graphs Privacy concerns around ensuring proper PII anonymization and cross-device correlation. User ID graphs can face similar regulatory concerns to what FLoC had — too precise and too invasive.Email-based user IDs or device-based IDs may not be a reliable foundation for assembling user identities.
