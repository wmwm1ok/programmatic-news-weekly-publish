一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • CTV 正在从品牌曝光媒体转向可归因、可按结果优化的效果渠道。例如 Singular 与 Roku 强化 CTV 归因，tvScientific 推按结果收费，Vibe 持续教育广告主用业务指标判断 CTV 成效。
  • 流媒体库存供给继续向 FAST 与高端视频两端扩张，发布商和平台都在强化广告变现基础设施。例如 Business Insider 选择 Magnite 推 FAST，wedotv 借 Samsung TV Plus 入印度，FreeWheel 与 TF1 强调长期 Premium Video 变现。
  • 零售媒体正从站内广告位销售升级为内容、电视归因和增量衡量结合的商业闭环。例如 Walmart Connect 强调内容带动销售，Kroger 连接电视广告与结账数据，NIQ 聚焦零售媒体增量测试。
  • 后 Cookie 时代的数据竞争焦点转向跨平台受众激活、数据质量和隐私合规的平衡。例如 Lotame 推跨 DSP 受众激活，Truthset 接入 Yahoo DSP 评分受众，OpenX 讨论儿童隐私合规风险。
  • 广告平台正在把 AI 与自动化嵌入创意、投放和验证流程，以降低运营复杂度并提升决策速度。例如 Amazon Ads 上线多项 AI 功能，DoubleVerify 将 DV Neura 嵌入代理流程，Smartly 聚焦 CTV 创意自动化。
  • CTV is moving from a reach-led branding medium toward an attributable, outcome-optimized performance channel. Signals include Singular’s Roku partnership for CTV attribution, tvScientific’s outcome-based pricing, and Vibe’s guidance on measuring CTV with business KPIs.
  • Streaming supply is expanding across both FAST and premium video, while publishers and platforms invest in ad monetization infrastructure. Examples include Business Insider choosing Magnite for FAST, wedotv entering India via Samsung TV Plus, and FreeWheel highlighting long-term premium video monetization with TF1.
  • Retail media is evolving beyond onsite ad placements into a closed loop of content, TV attribution, and incrementality measurement. Walmart Connect highlighted sales lift from branded content, Kroger tied TV ads to checkout data, and NIQ focused on retail media incrementality testing.
  • In the post-cookie market, data competition is shifting toward cross-platform audience activation, data quality, and privacy compliance. Lotame promoted cross-DSP audience activation, Truthset brought rated audiences to Yahoo DSP, and OpenX addressed children’s privacy risks.
  • Ad platforms are embedding AI and automation into creative, buying, and verification workflows to reduce operational friction and speed up decisions. Amazon Ads launched new AI features, DoubleVerify embedded DV Neura into agency workflows, and Smartly focused on CTV creative automation.

However, alternative solutions, labeled under the umbrella terms 'Universal IDs' and 'Alternative IDs,' have started to proliferate that work across multiple SSPs networks, enabling the identification of individual users more persistently than current, SSP-partitioned identification. 9 In order to compare proposals to existing cookie-based solutions, we have selected what we consider to be the three primary alternative identifier solutions - 'The Trade Desk Unified ID 2. 0 (UID 2. 0),' 'LiveRamp RampID,' and 'Secure Web Addressability Network (SWAN). ' Our selection was informed by our reading of trade publications covering the digital advertising industry and the frequency of reporting on specific solutions. We have incorporated the reporting into the data we have collected along with messaging and technical documentation that the authors of the different solutions have made public. Hence, based on our suggested tracking specifications in section 3, we analyze each of the three main cookie-less tracking solutions discussed by the industry. In terms of the instrument of user identification, the three solutions vary. For SWAN, an individual is identified when they first visit a publisher site that has adopted the solution (Asim, 2022; Schiff, 2021d; Thomson & Rescorla, 2021). Upon loading the publisher page, the user is presented with a pop-up asking for consent to show personalized advertising on the current site and other sites that have adopted the solution. As part of the pop-up, the user also has the option to share their email address which can serve as an identifier.
