一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • 零售媒体正在加速向 CTV 和社交流量外延扩张，Walmart 通过收购 Vibe.co 补强 Walmart Connect 的流媒体广告技术栈，Kroger Precision Marketing 则接入 TikTok 自助广告，把零售数据从站内购物场景延伸到大屏和社交触点。
  • AI 正从广告投放辅助工具升级为程序化交易和营销数据底座，OpenX 推出 OpenX IQ 优化供应侧决策，PubMatic 用 AgenticOS 对测传统 DSP 提升触达效率，Zeta Global 与 Palantir 共建以 Athena by Zeta 为核心的营销 AI 基础设施。
  • 独立测量和媒体质量验证的重要性继续上升，AppsFlyer 获 Moloco、Google、Meta 和 Unity 投资以推进独立广告测量，DoubleVerify 将 DV Authentic AdVantage 扩展至 Meta 和 TikTok，Tenjin 推出 Site ID Optimization Tool 帮助移动广告主控制流量质量与欺诈风险。
  • CTV 广告进入规模化竞争后，平台方同时面对库存扩张、定向增强和隐私合规压力，Amazon DSP 接入 Comscore Proximic 人群强化 CTV/Web/移动定向，FreeWheel 持续讨论 AI 在电视广告交易中的应用，而 Roku 与佛州总检察长达成隐私和解凸显 CTV 数据治理成为平台经营约束。
  • 大型平台广告产品正向更自动化、更强准入控制演进，Amazon Ads 在 Seller Central 推出 Advertising Campaign Manager 并展示 Alexa+ Agentic Ads，Google Ads 更新 Demand Gen 并扩大欧洲金融广告验证，显示平台一方面提升自动化投放能力，另一方面强化行业合规门槛。
  • Retail media is extending aggressively into CTV and social inventory: Walmart is acquiring Vibe.co to strengthen Walmart Connect’s streaming ad stack, while Kroger Precision Marketing is integrating with TikTok self-service ads to bring retail data beyond owned commerce environments into big-screen and social touchpoints.
  • AI is moving from a campaign-assistance layer to the core infrastructure for programmatic trading and marketing data: OpenX launched OpenX IQ for supply-side decisioning, PubMatic tested AgenticOS against an incumbent DSP to improve reach efficiency, and Zeta Global partnered with Palantir to build an AI data foundation centered on Athena by Zeta.
  • Independent measurement and media-quality verification are becoming more strategic: AppsFlyer received investment from Moloco, Google, Meta and Unity to advance independent ad measurement, DoubleVerify expanded DV Authentic AdVantage to Meta and TikTok, and Tenjin launched a Site ID Optimization Tool to help mobile advertisers manage traffic quality and fraud risk.
  • As CTV advertising scales, platforms are balancing inventory growth, stronger targeting and privacy compliance: Amazon DSP is adding Comscore Proximic audiences across CTV, web and mobile, FreeWheel is framing AI use cases for TV ad transactions, and Roku’s privacy settlement with the Florida attorney general shows data governance is becoming a direct operating constraint for CTV platforms.
  • Major ad platforms are evolving toward more automation and tighter eligibility controls: Amazon Ads launched Advertising Campaign Manager in Seller Central and showcased Alexa+ Agentic Ads, while Google Ads updated Demand Gen and expanded financial advertiser verification in Europe, signaling both more automated buying tools and stricter category compliance.

印度 CTV 广告升至主流媒体计划 CTV moves into mainstream media plans, but measurement remains the missing link

报道称印度 CTV 广告已从实验性触达层进入主流媒体计划，市场规模约 3,000 crore 卢比；但预算迁移后，跨屏测量与效果证明仍是核心短板。

Connected TV has moved quickly from an experimental reach layer to a Rs 3,000-crore advertising market in India. But as budgets follow audiences to the large streaming screen, a sharper question is emerging: can the industry measure CTV with the same confidence with which it sells it? For advertisers, the promise is compelling — television-like impact, digital-style targeting and access to premium, connected households. The gap lies in fragmented measurement across linear TV, OTT apps, smart-TV interfaces, OEM environments, YouTube, programmatic pipes and walled gardens. That gap is now defining the next phase of the market. As CTV becomes part of annual video planning, brands are no longer asking only whether it delivers reach. They want to know where the ad appeared, how often the same household was exposed, and whether the exposure moved business outcomes. Measurement moves to the centre Swagatika Das, CEO & Co-founder, Nat Habit, said India is increasingly becoming an “AND market” rather than an “OR market”, where consumers engage with both television and digital ecosystems simultaneously. “While CTV offers a premium viewing environment and highly engaged audiences, advertisers still need stronger visibility into how exposure translates into business outcomes. Frequency management across screens is another challenge,” she said, adding that brands will expect unified measurement, better audience transparency and stronger attribution.

CTV 观看时长增 8%，美国家庭智能电视达 82% CTV gains 8% viewing time as smart TVs reach 82% of US homes

DISQO 相关数据显示，CTV 观看时长增长 8%，智能电视覆盖 82% 美国家庭；看到电视广告后，39% 的行动在立即或数分钟内发生，24% 在当天稍晚发生。

The timing data from the same DISQO source shows that 39% of actions happen immediately or within minutes of seeing the TV ad. Another 24% occur later the same day, 13% within a few days, and 5% within a week. In aggregate, 81% of consumers took action within one week of seeing a TV ad. These figures directly matter to search and commerce-focused buyers. The 46% who search online after seeing a TV ad represent a direct, measurable linkage between television exposure and search query behavior - the same connection thatPPC Land has tracked through CTV-to-search attribution developments, including the Freewheel, OpenX/TVision, and measurement infrastructure updates covered across 2025 and early 2026. Contextual implications for media planning The VAB report does not prescribe budget allocations, but the data it assembles maps the television screen as the dominant surface for video ad delivery while framing smartphones as the device where downstream action takes place. That architecture - television exposure leading to mobile action - is not new as a concept, but the specific figures update what practitioners can use to defend or challenge planning assumptions. Connected TV at 86% of digital ad views and 2 hours and 37 minutes of daily viewing gives the large screen a structural claim on video budgets that smaller-format alternatives face difficulty matching on reach alone. The 53% cross-device action rate from DISQO adds an outcome dimension: the TV screen initiates behavior that completes elsewhere, usually on mobile, usually within hours.
