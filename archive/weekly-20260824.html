一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • CTV 正从品牌曝光渠道加速转向可验证的效果与新客增长渠道。例如 Amazon Ads 与 Roku 用 400 多个案例证明 CTV 带动销售，Roku 以 CPM 保障争夺 DTC 预算，Vibe 接入 Omnisend 打通广告与转化运营。
  • 零售媒体正在从零售商变现工具升级为全漏斗营销基础设施，同时进入标准化与精细化运营阶段。例如 Walmart 广告业务高增长，PepsiCo 将 Walmart Connect 用作新品发布媒体，ANA 推进 RMN 测量标准化。
  • AI 正在重塑消费者发现、搜索与购买路径，但广告产品化仍受信任、测量和归因约束。例如 Basis 将 AI 视为新媒介渠道，Microsoft Advertising 讨论 AI 代购可见性，Digiday 指出 AI 代理广告存在测量难题。
  • 后 Cookie 时代的竞争焦点转向可验证身份、上下文信号和合规供给，而非单一路线替代第三方 Cookie。例如 LiveRamp 推进 ATS，MGID 更新后 Cookie 策略，myGaru 以电信身份方案补充浏览器信号。
  • 程序化交易正更强调高质量供给、数据可验证性和业务结果，低质流量与廉价数据的容忍度下降。例如 OpenXSelect 强化供给侧定向，FreeWheel 聚焦高端程序化视频，Lotame 警示免费受众数据隐性成本。
  • CTV is moving from a brand-reach channel toward a measurable performance and new-customer acquisition channel. For example, Amazon Ads and Roku analyzed 400+ cases linking CTV to sales, Roku is using CPM guarantees to win DTC budgets, and Vibe integrated Omnisend to connect ads with conversion operations.
  • Retail media is evolving from retailer monetization into full-funnel marketing infrastructure, while also entering a phase of standardization and operational refinement. For example, Walmart reported strong ads growth, PepsiCo positioned Walmart Connect as launch media, and ANA advanced RMN measurement standards.
  • AI is reshaping consumer discovery, search and purchase journeys, but ad productization is still constrained by trust, measurement and attribution. For example, Basis frames AI as a new media channel, Microsoft Advertising discusses visibility in AI shopping, and Digiday highlights measurement challenges for ads shown to AI agents.
  • In the post-cookie market, competition is shifting toward verifiable identity, contextual signals and compliant supply rather than a single replacement for third-party cookies. For example, LiveRamp is promoting ATS, MGID updated its post-cookie strategy, and myGaru proposes telecom identity as a browser-signal alternative.
  • Programmatic trading is placing more value on premium supply, verifiable data and business outcomes, reducing tolerance for low-quality traffic and cheap audience data. For example, OpenXSelect emphasizes supply-side targeting, FreeWheel focuses on premium programmatic video, and Lotame warns about the hidden cost of free audience data.

The industry is shifting toward first-party data strategies and contextual advertising as the practical path forward. 文章介绍myGaru一类电信身份方案：通过移动网络运营商数据解析消费者身份，而非依赖浏览器Cookie信号。此类方案面向无Cookie广告定向与归因场景。

myGaru’s model, broadly typical of telecom-based identity plays, resolves consumer identity through mobile network operator data rather than browser signals. Carriers already know who a subscriber is (verified at sign-up), what device they use, and increasingly, with consent, patterns of app and web behavior across that device. Package that into a privacy-compliant identity layer, and you get something advertisers can plug into programmatic pipes without touching cookies at all.This isn’t new in concept. Telcos have flirted with ad tech for over a decade (remember Verizon’s AOL/Yahoo experiment?). What’s different now is timing. Brands are desperate for durable identity, regulators have made consent infrastructure mandatory anyway, and telcos have finally built the consent-management layers needed to monetize their data responsibly.Subscriber identity is verified at contract signup, not self-reported.Carrier-level data persists across apps, browsers, and devices tied to one account.Consent can be captured once, at the network level, rather than app-by-app.Cross-border roaming data adds a layer travel and hospitality brands can’t get elsewhere.Why This Matters More Than Another Cookie ReplacementMost cookieless solutions try to recreate what cookies did. Telecom identity does something structurally different: it starts from a verified real-world identity and works outward. That distinction matters for brand teams thinking aboutidentity resolutionas a foundation for AI-driven media buying, not just a patch for lost targeting.
