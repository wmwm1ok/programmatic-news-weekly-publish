一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • CTV 正从品牌曝光渠道升级为可编排、可衡量的效果媒体。例如 Teads 推出 CTV Ensemble，Vibe 接入 Lifesight 衡量，Google Ads 用视频广告系列组统一优化触达频次。
  • 广告 AI 的竞争焦点正从单点生成转向运营工作流和数据底座。例如 Singular 强调 AI-ready 数据基础，Tenjin 推出 MCP Server，Teads 预告 MCP 集成，同时 Clinch、Innovid 提醒旧流程直接叠加 AI 存在落地缺口。
  • 零售媒体正在向统一投放管理和增量测量深化，而不只卖站内流量。例如 Shirofune 接入 Walmart Connect，Walmart Connect 讨论交易数据驱动的增量测量，行业报告也指出 RMN 与 DSP 集成会进一步加深。
  • 隐私与身份基础设施仍在重塑程序化广告的可寻址能力。例如 FTC 起诉 Kochava 涉嫌出售敏感位置数据，Programmatic Spain 对比多种 ID 方案，无 Cookie 与 Privacy Sandbox 指南继续推动 DSP 适配新机制。
  • 平台正把优质 IP、体育和沉浸式体验作为广告增量入口。例如 TikTok 与 NBA、WNBA 达成全球合作，Roku 将 Roku City 延展为现实体验，Clinch 借世界杯讨论大规模直播广告基础设施缺口。
  • CTV is evolving from a brand-reach channel into an orchestrated and measurable performance medium. Signals include Teads launching CTV Ensemble, Vibe integrating Lifesight measurement, and Google Ads using video campaign groups to optimize reach and frequency across campaigns.
  • Ad AI competition is shifting from point-solution generation to workflow automation and data infrastructure. Singular is positioning AI-ready marketing data foundations, Tenjin launched an MCP Server, Teads previewed MCP integration, while Clinch and Innovid warned that bolting AI onto broken processes creates execution gaps.
  • Retail media is deepening into unified campaign management and incrementality measurement, beyond simply selling onsite inventory. Shirofune integrated Walmart Connect, Walmart Connect highlighted transaction-data-based incrementality, and market reports point to deeper RMN-DSP integration.
  • Privacy and identity infrastructure continues to reshape addressability in programmatic advertising. The FTC sued Kochava over sensitive location data, Programmatic Spain compared multiple ID frameworks, and cookieless and Privacy Sandbox guides show DSPs are still adapting to new mechanisms.
  • Platforms are using premium IP, sports and immersive experiences as new advertising growth surfaces. TikTok partnered globally with the NBA and WNBA, Roku extended Roku City into real-world experiences, and Clinch used the World Cup to highlight infrastructure gaps for live advertising at scale.

Demand-side platforms (DSPs)install a UID2RTB SDK Clientto decrypt tokens. Then register with UID2 Administrator to receive anauthkeyused to initialize the SDK. Once done, a DSP can call the SDK during RTB auctions to decrypt a UID2 token into a UID2 identifier. The SDK sends over the UID2 along with a timestamp of when a user first established the UID2 — and notifies if the token is expired. This mechanism allows DSPs to match audience data and conduct frequency capping. The DSP will also need to install a mechanism for honoring user opt-out requests, sent by the UID2 Administrator. Data management platforms (DMPs) and data providerswill have to supply audience data based on UID2 to DSPs. The mechanics are similar to the way that data providers push data sets using a cookie or device ID. The DSP then reconciles UID2 in the pushed data sets with one present in the incoming bid request to target users.
