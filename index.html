一、本周核心趋势1. Key Trends This Week

  • 跨渠道测量正成为零售媒体和CTV的基础能力——广告主要求把曝光、增量和零售转化放进同一评估框架。例如Singular支持Walmart Connect与tvScientific衡量，Vibe强调CTV与MMM兼容，Amazon DSP新增零售商转化归因。
  • CTV正在从品牌曝光库存升级为可定向、可归因、可优化的效果渠道。例如Smartly联手Roku引入社交式定向，Viant接入Publica拓展直连CTV供应，Vibe案例强调ROAS与绩效流媒体投放。
  • 大型广告平台正把更多媒体形态纳入统一购买与频控体系，降低跨屏投放碎片化。例如Amazon DSP开放Spotify播客库存，Google Ads推出视频Campaign Groups频控，Kroger强化自助式Ad Platform能力。
  • 隐私与身份方案重心正从浏览器替代技术转向第一方数据和可追踪触点闭环。例如Google叫停Privacy Sandbox，Kochava推出二维码归因平台，LiveRamp为Netflix开展品牌投放，Amazon加强零售转化归因。
  • Cross-channel measurement is becoming core infrastructure for retail media and CTV, as advertisers want impressions, incrementality and retail outcomes in one framework. For example, Singular added Walmart Connect and tvScientific measurement, Vibe highlighted CTV-MMM compatibility, and Amazon DSP added retailer-level conversion attribution.
  • CTV is moving from an awareness inventory pool to a targetable, attributable and optimizable performance channel. For example, Smartly partnered with Roku on social-style targeting, Viant integrated with Publica for direct CTV access, and Vibe highlighted ROAS-driven streaming campaigns.
  • Major ad platforms are folding more media formats into unified buying and frequency-management systems to reduce cross-screen fragmentation. For example, Amazon DSP opened Spotify podcast inventory, Google Ads added video Campaign Groups frequency controls, and Kroger expanded its self-serve Ad Platform capabilities.
  • Privacy and identity strategies are shifting away from browser-replacement technology toward first-party data and measurable touchpoint loops. For example, Google reportedly halted Privacy Sandbox, Kochava launched a QR attribution platform, LiveRamp ran a Netflix brand campaign, and Amazon strengthened retail conversion attribution.

Google 叫停 Privacy Sandbox 计划 Programmatic of Tomorrow or a DSP Utopia: Privacy Sandbox Explained

文章称 Google 已正式终止 Privacy Sandbox 这一为 Chrome 设计的第三方 Cookie 替代方案，意味着隐私广告与浏览器内定向路线将进一步调整。

TL;DR: The Privacy Sandbox was Google's six-year project to replace third-party cookies with privacy-preserving ad technologies built into the Google Chrome browser. Google officially discontinued the initiative in October 2025 after retiring most of its core APIs, including Topics, Protected Audience, and Attribution Reporting, citing low adoption and continued regulatory pressure. Third-party cookies will remain in Chrome indefinitely, but privacy compliance obligations under GDPR and CCPA remain unchanged. The industry is shifting toward first-party data strategies and contextual advertising as the practical path forward. For DSPs, the programmatic buying mechanics that existed before the Privacy Sandbox will continue to operate, but advertisers and publishers need to invest in their own data infrastructure rather than waiting for a platform-level solution that no longer exists. "We've made the decision to maintain our current approach to offering users third-party cookie choice in Chrome."That was Google's statement on April 22, 2025. Six months later, on October 17, 2025, Google retired most of the Privacy Sandbox APIs it had spent six years building. The initiative launched in 2019 with a clear goal: replace cross-site tracking with privacy-preserving alternatives that keptad techand digital advertising functional. It ended with a quiet blog post, a long retirement list of APIs, and an industry largely back where it started. According to aMarch 2025 Deloitte report, only15%of global marketers felt fully prepared for a cookieless environment, despite years of warnings. The Privacy Sandbox was supposed to solve that. It did not.
